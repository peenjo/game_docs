### Threshold Game Updates
Harry explained recent supernatural events and character backgrounds to players who missed previous sessions. Harry demonstrated new game features including lockers where players can view and interact with their belongings, and showed off AI-generated art for the voodoo-themed content. The players tested out the new inventory system and discussed their character backgrounds, with Clem working on story updates and Jackson struggling to reconcile certain character options related to cult background. 

### Adrian Kwan
The group received a call from Adrian Kwan, a representative of the Street Queen. He said he had something for the group to investigate and to meet him at the NightWire bar. The Street Queen had gotten information that there are supernatural occurrences in a tenement building where there was a recent fire in one of the apartments. The wife (Mara) and two children (Lena and Noah) are missing. Husband is a Union soldier who is alibied by his friends who were all on a hunting trip.

Adrian provides police and fire department documents for the group to read. None of them mention any supernatural events. There do seem to be inconsistencies in the story; the group suspects the husband. They decide to visit the scene to investigate potential supernatural elements, particularly since neighbors reported supernatural phenomena before the fire. The group exchanges contact information with Adrian, who also provided Do Not Detain papers to facilitate going through checkpoints. Adrian said he should be the first phone call if they are detained.

### Fire Damage Investigation
The group examined the apartment and found evidence of arson, with diesel being splashed along walls in a pattern suggesting someone had deliberately set the fire. During their investigation, they discovered that some clothes appeared to be missing, possibly indicating the family was kidnapped rather than leaving voluntarily. Some of the team then decided to remove their anchors (magic protective devices) to better sense supernatural presence before interviewing neighbors about any unusual activities. They saw nothing different in the apartment.

### Evelyn
They interviewed Evelyn, an elderly woman who lives across the hall. She described seeing unusual phenomena and hearing neighbors' arguments before the fire, though she didn't see the missing Mercer family members who were reportedly on vacation. The group members not wearing anchors saw that Evelyn had a ghost on her back, who disappeared when it noticed them. The guessed it was Noah. Evelyn said she has been feeling tired a lot recently.

### Navarro Family
When they attempted to interview the Navarro family next door, they had the door slammed in their face. Clem listened through the door but got no useful information. The group decided to come back later to after gathering information about ghosts.

### Ghost Investigation Apartment Building
Approaching the apartment building the group observed two ghostly presences in the window, what appeared to be the mother and daughter. They theorized that all three family members were likely murdered, with the husband potentially killing them and disposing of the bodies during a hunting trip. 

Clem attempted to summon the spirit of Mara Mercer using Veve rituals and channeling techniques. During the ritual, Mara appeared with her daughter Lena. Mara would not speak until the men had left the area. She then led Clem through threshold doorway, leaving the group unable to follow. After the spirit encounter, they discovered Clem's unconscious body hidden under a blanket and trash, indicating she had crossed over to the spirit world. 

### Limen
After leading Clem to the Limen, both the spirits attacked her, although they were not effective. Clem defended herself, but was also not effective. After some attempts to persuade the spirits, Clem decided to escape and successfully navigated through a threshold and return to her physical body.

After Clem had recovered a bit, she described the incident and mentioned that the apartment in the spirit world appeared unburned despite the reported fire. The team decided to regroup and head home rather than continue investigating that night, though they agreed to learn the Spirit Gate ritual from Clem to allow future ghost investigations. Victor volunteered to lead the next investigation due to his melee skills and willpower, with Clem providing guidance on the ritual.

### Spirit World Investigation
The group returned to the apartment building to conduct a spirit world investigation Victor, Blake, and Brian through a ritual to enter the spirit mundus. They explored a burned apartment that appeared undamaged in the spirit world and discovered clues including divorce papers and a family photo from Bear Creek cabin, though they didn't see any ghosts. After they returned to the physical world they encountered Mr Navarro with a shotgun in the hallway. Victor attempted to persuade him but failed. Navarro fled back to his apartment when he saw the team pointing all their guns at him. The team concluded that the apartment fire was likely a cover-up for a murder, and they decided to investigate the Bear Creek cabin next.

### Bear Creek Cabin Investigation 
The group planned to meet at 8:30 AM the next morning to drive to the cabin location. After learning that Elias Mercer had left the officer's quarters an hour ahead of them, they agreed to proceed to the cabin while being cautious about potentially encountering Mercer and his soldier friends.

Through phone calls to Adrian, they leared that Sgt O'Rourke had also left the base and was unaccounted for, but they had an hour head start. Once they arrived in Bear Creek, they did not have any specific information about the location of the cabin. Brian failed to charm the general store owner to provide the information. Eventually one of the group found a phone book and got the street address.

### The Cabin
The group got near, parked and proceeded on foot to the cabin. Elias did not see them, and was outside with a rifle in his hands. Blake successfully shot Elias in the foot using a hunting rifle, causing him to fall and bleed heavily. The team then searched the cabin and discovered evidence including a video camera setup and various items in a basement room. Clem attempted to use a magical healing spell called "Flesh of Clay" to treat Elias, with Jackson providing first aid assistance, though the procedure caused additional damage before stopping the bleeding. Oopsie.

### Cabin Defense 
The group heard a car approaching the cabin, assumed it was Danny O'Rourke with any number of squad mates. They took up positions in the cabin.

O'Rourke approached the cabin alone and called out. Clem responded, telling him she was the cleaning lady and she did not know where Elias (unconscious inside the house) was. O'Rourke approached the door, but saw Blake in the window with the gun and went for cover behind the car. Four other squad members revealed themselves and started approaching the cabin and firing.

Victor successfully hit a squaddie with a pistol, while Jackson was heavily injured by an enemy's high damage roll despite being in cover. The opinion was that there was nothing to be done for Jackson, so Victor chose to maintain his aim rather than attempt first aid on Jackson.

Blake successfully eliminated the enemy leader with a critical hit, which caused some of the squaddies to panic and run. Victor and Blake polished off the rest of the squaddies, although it was pretty close.

### Resolution
Jackson died, the squaddies were killed or ran away. Blake emerged as MVP after delivering the final killing blow with a critical headshot. Several players sustained injuries during the battle, with Brian and Clem requiring first aid from Victor to stabilize them until they could get to real medical treatment.

The group found video evidence of the imprisonment and torture of the family by all of the soldiers, which they will turn over to the authorities.
