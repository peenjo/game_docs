### Threshold RPG Character Creation
The group discussed character creation for a Threshold Horror RPG game, with Harry helping players set up their character images and descriptions. Julius created a character named Blake Evans described as a Canadian with low intelligence, while Stevie described his character Jackson Sallow as a skinny, country bumpkin type. The players discussed various character details including clothing, backgrounds, and physical appearances, with some expressing concerns about repetitive or unflattering character images generated by the AI. Harry mentioned he had made significant system improvements including giving Eric full GM status and implementing new dice rolling options, though he noted he doesn't back up the game data while Eric rolled his eyes again.

### Supernatural Veil and Reality Strata
Harry explained to the group that they had experienced supernatural events that made them aware of the veil between the natural and supernatural worlds, classifying them as "adrift" or "walkers." He described how the veil protects people from supernatural sights and explained the six strata of reality including Mundus, Limen, Purgatoria, Infernum, Abyss, and Primordia. The group learned that while wearing enchanted necklaces called anchors, they would be temporarily protected from supernatural perception, but could choose to interact with the supernatural world without the protection if they remove their anchor.

### Supernatural Training and Recovery Program
Harry explained that supernatural creatures like vampires and werewolves exist, though they avoid most populated areas except for vampires who find cities interesting. He outlined the multi-month recovery and training program for the group, starting with psychological recovery and learning to function normally while wearing the protective anchors. The program progressed through understanding ghosts, possessions, and glamours, with practical survival training in weeks 5-6, and includes information about the Foundation which provides apartments and job assistance.

### Supernatural World and Game Systems
Harry explained the supernatural world to the group, covering topics like using iron against ghosts, silver against vampires and werewolves, and the criminal underworld in Toronto where supernatural beings operate. He introduced key concepts including operators (criminals unaware of the supernatural), hunters (operators with a "Kill them all" approach), and walkers (aware supernatural combatants), as well as various brokers and markets where jobs can be found. 

### Toronto Journey
The group discussed their upcoming journey to Toronto, where they will be introduced to new identities and employment. They learned about the differences between Templars and Hospitaliers, with Templars protecting refugees between safe havens and Hospitaliers protecting the havens themselves. The discussion covered their accommodation in Toronto, with Tom Bennett (the group's Guide) arranging fake identities and providing a stipend, and they explored different districts of the city including Scarborough as a potential living location. The conversation ended with a discussion about job opportunities, though specific employment choices were not finalized. Except for Victor, who was given a job at the University of Toronto Library so he could continue his research.

### Nightwire Bar, Naomi Patch
Tom brought the group to the the Nightwire bar to meet Naomi Patch, a broker who handles jobs for walkers. They exchanged contact information and she will reach out if she has a job that suits them.They also described the night market, a weekly event where characters can purchase cyberware, and introduced Switch, a character who meets the group there. The discussion included details about the dangerous nature of the characters' work and their limited initial resources of 1,000 crowns.

### Midnight Market, Switch 
The group then visited the Midnight Market, the mobile black market that operates every Saturday night. They met Switch, the broker that conducts business at the market. They addressed technical issues with accessing some of the shops and managing inventory. The discussion included pricing concerns about market items, particularly the **high cost of backpacks and other equipment, which Harry acknowledged needed review**. Harry mentioned that a random item list would be created for weekly market updates.

### Steak and Cask Biker Bar, Mara
The next night Tom took the group far out of town to the Steak and Cask, a bar where operators socialize, noting that while it appears to be a motorcycle club, it's not a gang and has rules against gang affiliations. They were introduced to Mara, the broker there who explained that the Black Creek Road job is dangerous. The last group of walkers and multiple hunter groups have attempted to take it but none returned. She did not offer it to the group as they are too inexperienced. The group stayed to have some drinks and play some pool and get known a bit more.


### Raven's Loft, Sebastian Bale
The group then visited the Raven's Loft, a supernatural establishment in downtown Toronto where certain items like anchors, salt, iron, and silver are prohibited. Harry explained that while the venue is in a populated area, once outside the building, the veil would protect them from supernatural threats. The inside of Raven's Loft is "PeaceBound", which is a powerful curse that destroys anyone or anything that starts a fight, effectively making it neutral ground. During their visit, they met Corvin, the owner who is a Reaper and Sebastian Bale the broker. Sebastian suggested they not stay, warning them about the dangers of drawing attention from the other patrons due to their fresh presence in Toronto's supernatural world. The group decided to follow Sebastian's advice and leave the establishment rather than risk staying longer.

### The Street Queen 
On the way from Raven's Loft to their homes, Jackson decide to remove his anchor. Near the destination, they noticed an old woman with a cart notice them. She spoke with Tom and said the group need to see her the next day. It turns out she is the street queen, a powerful leader of the homeless and undercity population who controls thousands of followers. He emphasized that while she appears as a friendly old lady, she is not to be underestimated and maintains a reputation for reliability in the community.
