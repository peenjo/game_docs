### Threshold Development Updates
The group discussed updates to the role-playing game, including Harry's introduction of a new "hobbies" system that allows players to access specialized skills they otherwise couldn't obtain. Eric reported progress on automating combat special effects tables, with three out of six tables now updated. The team then recounted their previous week's adventure involving a missing library keeper, a stolen book about ghouls, and discovering that the book served as an impossible information conduit between the spiritual library world and the physical realm, similar to a USB drive.

### Riverside Deli Investigation
The group discussed investigating a deli that matched the description of an Amaranth facility. They entered the building with tethers on and observed that the windows appeared normal inside but turned black when tethers were removed. The group noted that the building gave them a creepy feeling, similar to being around psychopaths, though the people inside appeared normal. They decided to investigate the basement next, with Clem purchasing an amazing sandwich and attempting to strike up a conversation with the deli owner but failing to gather information.

### Investigation Legwork
The group discussed their previous assignments, including who hired them for different jobs and the similarities between buildings they were asked to watch. Jackson volunteered to obtain building plans from city offices the next day, despite the bureaucratic process requiring long waits and payment. The team then planned to meet at the LiveWire Cafe to speak with Patches about potential connections between their assignments. Later, they received a mysterious phone call from Corvin, a reaper, who invited them to the Raven's Loft at 11 PM to discuss an urgent matter.

### Vessel Distress Mission
Corvin told them that he had gone to clain a channeler's soul (his day job) when he was prevented from doing so. He had faint images of a ship in the water within 100km of the city which was in distress. The group contacted Marisol Vance and Mayor Jameson about the situation, and after speaking with Jameson, they learned about a missing container tanker called the MV Orpheus. The team was instructed to meet at McKinley Seaport where they would board a search and rescue aircraft to investigate.

### Cormorant Rescue Vehicle
The Seach and Rescue team was not happy about the group tagging along, but had to agree because they were ordered to. After a brief pissing contest between Captain Elina Marsh and Victor, everyone boarded the rescue ship.
The group discussed their experience flying in a high-tech cormorant rescue vehicle through a storm, which was little to none. Victor was actively sick during the trip. After about 30 minutes of turbulent flight, the vehicle approached what appeared to be an abandoned ship in the water and successfully transitioned to submersible mode.

### Ship Boarding Safety Procedure
The group discussed exploring a ship using a 3D display that shows different levels including deck, cargo hold, and bridge levels. Captain Marsh explained the process for boarding the ship, which involved using platforms, grapples, and ascenders to safely transfer from their submersible to the ship's deck during high seas. Victor expressed concerns about the safety and military aspects of the operation, leading to a brief disagreement with Elina about following proper procedures. The discussion ended with the team preparing to enter torpedo tubes for the water transfer to the ship's platforms.

### Vessel Search and Threat Protocol
The group discussed searching a vessel, with Captain Marsh explaining the protocol of searching bow to stern and bottom to top. Victor revealed there might be a potentially lethal and demonic threat on the vessel, which did not go over well. The team decided to stay together and go to the bridge to try powering up the ship. The team encountered blood at the doorway, suggesting a fight or death had occurred there, which caught their attention and led to Clem collecting blood samples for analysis (and general fun).

### Kitchen Combat
The group discussed strategy for approaching a dangerous situation, with Captain Marsh taking the lead and positioning team members in a line formation. When they encountered a hostile figure in a kitchen, Victor was attacked with a cleaver but managed to shoot the enemy multiple times, eventually "killing" him. Being a Deadite, that didn't last long. The combat encounter revealed details about weapon effectiveness, armor penetration, and action point usage in the game system.

### Dining Room
After seeing the cook come back to life, Captain Marsh had a mental breakdown. Clem decided to create a demon barrier circle in the dining room to protect the group, while other characters like Blake and Jackson moved to secure the corridor. The session ended with Jackson successfully shutting and locking a door to prevent the Deadite from getting out of the kitchen.

### Hallway Combat
Rhea was attacked by a Deadite while another the annoying cook was banging on the locked door. The Deadite successfully grappled her and started knawing on her toe. Various players shot it until it stopped. Victor (on advice of Clem) dismembered it with a machete. Clem took a demon hand as a trophy and Victor scattering the remains. 

Clem then discussed the nature of the demons, saying they act as an infection rather than individual entities, and that any wounds risk spreading the infection to those present. 

### Protection Circle Combat
Clem continued creating a demon-binding circle to protect against further demonic influence. A Deadite girl attacked everyone's favorite damage magnet Victor, with both Victor and Jackson successfully putting her down.

### The Bridge
After the attack on Rhea and the collaps of Captain Marsh, the S&R team decided enough was enough and they went back to the Comorant. The wardens decided to continue searching the ship, securing the area by locking the doors from the hallway and heading up the stairs to the bridge. Blake and Brian took point and heard voices behind the bridge door before it opened. They encountered a large demon creature in the bridge area and successfully defeated it with some amazing shooting. There were no other creatures on the bridge. 

The team discussed their strategy for finding and dealing with the channeler who caused the demonic infection on the ship, with 21 people on the manifest who possibly would be infected. There were some lifeboats deployed, so the team doesn't know if the infection got off the ship already. They decided to head to the cargo hold next.
