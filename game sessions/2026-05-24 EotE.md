#### Inventory Management rule discussion
The group shifted into a discussion about encumbrance rules and inventory management. Harry clarified that encumbrance represented bulk and awkwardness, not just weight, though the digital tools still caused confusion. The group agreed to keep playing while the exact mechanics were researched later. Harry later explained that players can store items in their character sheet or lockers on the ship. They addressed issues with how encumbrance calculations work in the digital interface, which differs from the rulebooks.

During the festivities, they spotted a mysterious Mandalorian figure whose presence immediately raised alarms. Unsure whether to confront or monitor the individual, the team chose caution.

With the scrambles family party fast approaching there the investigation of the mysterious mandalorian was put on hold.

#### Return to the Farm
Xaus and Jaysin agreed to accompany Jimmy to threaten the Richardsons into selling back the farm for a lower price, with Tarin choosing to stay behind. The group planned to meet at Jimmy's family farm first before approaching the Richardson property, which was now secured with warning signs and appeared abandoned with unused moisture extractors. The group discovere that the farm had been raided. Tarin called a colleague to learn about the farm's status and discovered it had been seized in a drug raid five weeks ago. The team debated different approaches to investigate, including using an underground passage from the barn to the house, with Xaus suggesting caution due to potential explosives in a meth lab operation. 
    
#### Secrets Beneath the Surface
The group ultimately decided to enter the farmhouse stealthily rather than using an explosive. Xaus successfully snuck into the house and discovered a butler droid named Caesar who was maintaining the property. Tarin confirmed that the property owners had been arrested and the farm was impounded, leading to a discussion about what to do with the droid. The team discovered evidence of space meth production equipment in the basement, though it appeared the illegal chemicals had been removed, suggesting a potential dirty cop situation. The droid claimed no one had visited the property in five weeks, despite a police raid occurring just a month ago. They discovered Caesar had a restraining bolt that prevented him from leaving the property, and Tarin would need to travel there to remove it safely. The group also learned about a Mandalorian individual hanging around a local party, which Rynn's boss was interested in investigating.
