# Corrupt Police?

Caesars memory being wiped and the Scrambles farmhouse not being searched during the police raid seemed suspicious so Rynn decided to call her law enforcement contacts in the area for any further information collected during the raid. The local sheriff disclosed that Yusan Lon had been the Special Agent in charge of the police raid on the farms. 

# Special Agent

Yusan Lon explained that his team were the ones that wiped the  droids memory and gave reasoning for not searching the Scrambles farm house. Additionally he revealed that he intended to use the Scrambles farm house as a safe house rather that putting it up for auction. This put Jimmys plans to regain ownership of the farm house in jeopardy. 

# New Group Member

The group conducted a search of the other farm (which HAD been searched in the police raid) and unsurprisingly didnt find anything. On the way back to the Scrambles Farm, the group discovered Caesar attempting to flee. Jimmy negotiated with Caesar and ultimately agreed to pay him 100 credits per month for his services. 

# Back at Sandport

Rynn visited the Marshals office to report the suspicious findings at the farms. Her suspension was lifted and she reported the groups findings to fellow marshal Slep Noxon. He revealed that he had been investigating the corrupt law enforcement officers and that Rynn was compromising his case by alerting the officers.

# Party crasher

Enroute to the scrambles family dinner party the group once again joined the street festival and ran into Gessen who challenged Jason for a rematch with live weapons. Xaus intervened and alerted Gessen to the presence of a Marshal ending the encounter. While at the dinner party an Imerial Class Star destroyer appeared above Sandport and unleashed Tie fighters and troop transports on the city causing a mass panic.

# Imperial Occupation

The group returned to the marshals office in an attempt to get a hold of the situation. The marshals office didn't provide answers. However the empire announced a blockade of the system commencing soon. The group used the opportunity to escape the system in a dramatic manouvre that earned them the full attention of the imperial interdiction force. The imperial inspection placed a tracker on the ship, which the group noticed but left in place. They decided to make for Gorloff station, suspecting that the imperial presence in the system was related to the pit they discovered there.
