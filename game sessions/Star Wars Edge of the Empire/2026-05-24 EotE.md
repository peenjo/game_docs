Everyone but Tarin agreed to accompany Jimmy to threaten the Richardsons into selling back the farm for a lower price, with Tarin choosing to stay behind and keep working on finding the fake Mandalorian. #### Secrets Beneath the Surface
The group ultimately decided to approach the farmhouse stealthily before using an explosive. Xaus successfully snuck into the house and discovered a butler droid named Caesar who was maintaining the property. Rynn confirmed that the property owners had been arrested and the farm was impounded, leading to a discussion about what to do with the droid. The team discovered evidence of 'space meth' production equipment in the basement, though it appeared the illegal chemicals had been removed during the raid. The droid claimed no one had visited the property in five weeks, despite a police raid occurring just over a month ago. They discovered Caesar had a restraining bolt that prevented him from leaving the property, and Tarin would need to travel there to remove it safely. Jimmy flew back to pick up Tarin, who started working on removing the restraining bolt and seeing if anything could be recovered from Caesar's memory.
