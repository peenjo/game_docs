#### Space Dock Planning Meeting
The group discussed their plans after arriving at a space dock on Gorloth Junction, a 40,000-person town with impressive cliffside views. They addressed ship repairs needed due to damage, with the work expected to take two hours and automatically charged to their organization. The team decided they would travel to orbit to access the interplanetary network and retrieve additional funds. They also reviewed an updated bounty for a target named Kreeg, noting that the information had recently gone system-wide and would be known to many bounty hunters in the area.
#### Bounty Target Capture Plan
The team discussed capturing a Anto Varn and decided to proceed with their plan to return to Gorloth Junction and contact him. They agreed to stun the target upon capture and transport him to Renn Baro's location rather than keeping him in town. The group also decided to purchase a tracker to monitor the target's movements and discussed the bounty distribution, with Zaus receiving the majority of the $100,000 reward while the others would split a smaller amount (Renn takes 1/2 of every bounty). Additionally, they noted the arrival of a Varn Industries transport ship at the space station, though they chose to proceed with their current plan rather than investigate this development.
#### Bounty Target Capture Decision
Another group captured Anto first. The captors were taking the bounty target to a building rather than to the spaceport, suggesting they might be trying to extract information before turning him in. The group debated whether to get involved, with some suggesting they should focus on obtaining their own bounties instead. They ultimately considered two options: abandoning the Anto situation and going to a cantina for the other bounties, or getting involved with the captured bounty target. The group also discussed the potential value of information the bounty target might have, which could be worth more than the $200,000 bounty itself.
#### Stealth Mission Planning Discussion
The group discussed a stealth mission involving listening at a door to gather information about a suspect named Krieg. They discovered the suspect might not actually be Krieg, as he claimed not to know about a bounty on his name. The team planned to acquire weapons, including ion grenades to disable droids, and decided to approach the situation cautiously. They also considered the possibility of involving local authorities or contacting the Varn ship about the CEO's whereabouts. 
#### Anto Rescue Mission Planning Meeting
The group discussed their plan to rescue a man named Anto held by a battle droid, potentially set up by his sister. They decided to first contact the sister's representative, Miran, at the Majestic Hotel before proceeding with the rescue. The team also planned to gather supplies, including stun and ion grenades, for the upcoming confrontation. Rynn, as the marshal, was tasked with meeting with Barn's representative at the hotel, while Jimmy and others were to handle shopping for equipment. The group agreed to prioritize the rescue mission before meeting with Barn.
