#### Cargo Hold of MV Orpheus
The session picked right up with the group in the cargo hold of the MV Orpheus. They heard moving sounds in the dark but saw nothing, despite repeated efforts to lighthouse some visibility. Victor suggested moving to one of the walls to prevent attacks from all sides. He also looked up, and noticed three Deadites on the ceiling, who attacked. Jackson took some damage and dropped his weapon, Blake had his toe crushed, which limited his movement. The group put down all of the attackers. After they were down, members of the group had fun continually blasting them into smaller bits. 

#### Ship Investigation and Threat Assessment
The group continued searching the ship for the channeler and encountered Corvin the Reaper sitting outside a summoning circle. It's always the last place you look... They broke the circle so that Corvin could enter and claim the soul of the dead channeler. They searched the corpse of the channeler, taking items like candles and one hand for Clem. The team identified several remaining threats including an unhandled cook in the kitchen and uncertainty about whether any crew members had launched lifeboats, with particular concern about Rhea potentially becoming a deadite.

#### Dangerous Chef Situation
The group discussed how to handle a dangerous chef character on a ship, ultimately deciding to create a magical protection circle in front of the door. This effectively kept the deadite in the kitchen and allows them to shoot through the open physical door. After another round of machete fun, they contacted the Comorant had the engineer return to help restart the engines of Orpheus. They also decided to restrain Rhea, a damaged crew member, for potential infectious risks, and they awaited the arrival of a Coast Guard cutter to assist with the situation.

#### Ship Infection Concerns
The group discussed concerns about a ship potentially being an infection vector and debated whether to destroy it, but Brian knew that Deadites require physical bodies to survive and the infection would likely be contained once they were all wiped out. After landing at Kendrick Base on the Cormorant, the team was arrested by Union Marshals and taken into custody at a military base. During the arrest, they requested phone calls, with Victor suggesting they contact the mayor.

#### Military Base Escape
Brian was the first person interrogated and was allowed a phone call. He spoke briefly to the Mayor, who hung up as soon as the Marshall tried to speak. A few hours later, a Lieutenant Camry helped them escape by unlocking their cells and leading them to the back gate where Marisol Vance, the deputy commissioner, was waiting with their equipment and weapons. As they were leaving, an armored vehicle with a chain gun blocked their path. A man got out of the car approached with five black letters addressed to each warden. After delivering the letters, the armored vehicle withdrew. The letters turned out to be invitations to a party held by the Keepers of the Black Library.

#### Keeper Bennett Investiture Party Planning
The group discussed attending a formal party for Keeper Bennett's investiture at Keeper Heinz's old place, with Harry confirming they would need formal attire. Given their current fugitive status, they decided to all go to Clem's house and figure out next steps. Blake got medical attentiona and a NuToe, so back to full mobility with a slightly lowered essence. 

The mayor called and explained that he had to get out fast after his number was known to the Union Marshall. In the future he would provide a burner number. He was also providing new identities with paperwork. The Mayor suggested that Brian not go home, and other members of the group should consider moving as well.

The team also discussed purchasing options for cybernetics and weapons, with Jackson planning to visit a shop on Saturday for pistol purchases and considering RF tags for gun tracking.

#### Keeper Promotion General Chit Chat
Turns out Thomas being promoted to keeper involves a surgical procedure to separate his nervous system from his brain. They chatted with Thomas and wife Kaley before the procedure. During the social gathering before the ceremony, Clem learned about buildings with black windows from Lucien, who confirmed there were two such buildings in Toronto and mentioned that access to black libraries in North America had been shut down, potentially as a security measure. The discussion revealed concerns about a growing problem with black window buildings, including the MV Orpheus, and the group identified potential leads including Astrid from the sanctuary and the Keepers for further investigation.

The group discussed accessing ancient information in libraries, with Astrid Voss explaining that Sanctuaries maintain human libraries but demonic libraries are copies of human works. They learned about a Road Warden named Iam(?) who might have information about black windows and could potentially help investigate, though Harry warned she comes from another time (over a thousand years old) and is an old-school warrior. The team also discussed concerns about black libraries being closed, including one in Chicago where an Angaros did not return, and considered speaking to the Keepers Miriam and Elias for more information.

Corvin shared that the deceased keeper Heinz was not surprised by his visit, suggesting Heinz may have known about his impending death. Miriam mentioned that Heinz wanted to discuss his concerns before his death, but she blew him off. 

After the procedure was finished, most guests left quickly. The team stayed to eat and drink as much as the could and chat with Jacob, one of the few guests remaining.

Jacob shared concerns about collective visions involving black windows appearing in dreams, noting that seers are having these visions repeatedly and even dreaming of entire cities turning black. The conversation concluded with discussions about potential religious guidance.
