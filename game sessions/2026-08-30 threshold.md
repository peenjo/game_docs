### Hospital Visit and Skill Planning
After being 'nearly dead' from the encounter with the sqaddies at the cabin, Jackson is in hospital for 8 weeks. While there, he receives a visit from The Street Queen who thanks him for his help and brings a fruit basket. She reads Robinson Crusoe to him. The street queen leaves keys to a new Jeep Rubicon at the bottom of the fruit basket, which Jackson cannot drive due to his current condition and the fact that he doesn't know how to drive.

### Shopping Trip
While Jackson is convalescing, the group goes shopping for weapons, armor and sundries. They clarified monthly income details, with each character earning around 2,000 crowns a month plus an additional 1,000 from "the mayor". Harry's estimates on cost of living is about 1,500 crowns a month. 

### Surveillance Mission
The group got a call from Patch who told them to meet her at the Nightwire Club. There they discussed a surveillance job involving a tower owned by Amaranth Corporation, a weapons dealer, for which they would be paid $10,000 for this seemingly straightforward 24-hour surveillance task. While in the bar they got the stink eye from a team of Hunters, who evidently had been passed over because the request was specifically for Wardens. They didn't take it well.

### Parking Lot
Exiting the club, the group noticed one of the hunters waiting for them. After spreading out, they noticed the rest of the group clearly waiting to ambush them. After some verbal threats on both sides, the wardens pulled out their guns and the hunters left.

### 24-Hour Building Surveillance Mission
Our intrepid wardens drove to the building and drove around it to get more information. There were two entrances/exits, on opposite sides of the building. They drove to the top floor of a parking lot to observe the main entrance. While there, some of the group removed their tethers and saw that the entire building was flat black - no light coming out or reflecting from the surface. The team debated whether to investigate further or simply report their observations, with Jackson emphasizing they should focus on watching rather than entering the building due to the high payment for just surveillance.

### Building Surveillance and Emergency Response
Victor and Clem moved to the roof of a civilian building to monitor the other entrance, while the others remained in the parking lot. On the way to the other building Victor called Patch to update her and to tell her about the parking lot incident with the hunters. 

### Courier Message
The surveilance was uneventful until they received a message from Patch via courier about an urgent situation at 1204 Hellington Gardens requiring immediate attention with a 20K payment. The group agreed to leave their current post and drive to the new location, with Jackson driving and picking up the others at the other building.

### The Monroe Apartment
They found the building and were let inside. Before they knocked on the door of the apartment they could hear a loud argument inside the apartment. The Monroes invited them inside. The team discovered that Father Muldoon, a Catholic priest, was in a bedroom conducting what appeared to be an exorcism, with sounds of shouting and violence coming from behind a closed door. The group attempted to enter the bedroom and offer help, but the priest refused, sent them out and locked the door.

### Demonic Possession 
While discussion next steps, the noises inside the bedroom reached a peak and then went quiet. After breaking down the locked door, they saw the daughter had broken free of her restraints and ripped the priest's assistant into two pieces. They witnessed the possessed girl jump out a window, fall 12 stories, land on the roof of a car (fortunately not Jackson's new Jeep), get up and run away. The parents explained that their daughter had been experiencing unusual behavior for a day, including strange voices and fits, before they called in a Catholic priest for help. He was not up to the task, and was barely coherent after the attempt. The group did learn that the daughter was becoming a vampire.

### Vampire Possession
The group discussed how to handle a vampire possession situation where a daughter has been possessed and a demon was released through a window. Harry explained that vampires are strong but can be restrained with silver items like handcuffs, and the group obtained holy water from the priest to help combat the vampire. The team planned to follow the vampire's direction toward the lake while preparing for the confrontation, though they noted they had limited resources like silver bullets or specialized weapons. The group left the scene to pursue the possessed daughter while advising the family to seek help from influential contacts regarding the dead assistant in their bedroom. They aren't getting a refund on their security deposit.

### Vampire Demon Encounter Strategy
The group followed a blood trail to a partially constructed 20-story building. They discussed various strategies for trapping the creature, including using magic circles and baiting it with humans, but ultimately decided to follow it up the stairs, hoping to corner it and overpower it with the whole group. On the way up they heard a vehicle outside and saw the Boilermaker crew get out of a van with lots of guns.

### New plan
Let the vampire get past them and straight into the morons who followed them. Surprisingly that worked, and the vampire went through them like butter. The Veil was invoked because 'normals' saw something supernatural: the vampire burst into flames, but that didn't seem to slow her down much.

### Vampire Pursuit 
The group pursued the vampire away from the building and through the streets, but eventually lost the trail after being delayed by a police patrol. The team had to hide their weapons when police showed up but were otherwise not detained. After returning to their car, they decided to leave a voicemail for Patch to report their failed mission. She told them to meet at the Raven's Loft the next day.

### Raven's Loft Meeting
They encountered five figures at the bar, including Corvin the owner, Deputy Chief of Police Marisol Vance, "Mayor" Jameson, Lucien Vale, and Nadia Care. Nadia was the newly created vampire, Lucien the head vampire in the city. The conversation ended with an agreement that Nadia would join the vampire society under Lucien's responsibility, and the wardens would receive additional compensation for their services. The girl's family would be compensated 1 million crowns for the theft of their daughter.

After the meeting was over, they stayed and got free drinks while chatting with Corvin. Vance gave them information about a farm that supplies hunting equipment to hunters outside the city. The prices and inventory are better than the Midnight Market.
