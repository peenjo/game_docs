### Game XP System Discussion
Harry discussed the game's XP system and skill progression mechanics with the players. He explained that buying skills costs 100 XPs for level 0 skills, with OPR skills automatically providing level 2, and that players must complete their current career path before advancing to the next level for 100 XPs. Harry also mentioned he would provide Clem with a separate voodoo spells document and noted that players who didn't participate in the previous session didn't earn XP but still received 10,000 crowns. The discussion concluded with Harry offering to take the players to The Midnight Market or The Farm for equipment purchases.

### The Farm
The group met Bob, the guy who runs a store with various 'special' items for sale, including silver rounds and holy water. They clarified the pricing and quantities of items available at the farm store, with silver rounds costing 2,000 crowns for 100 rounds and holy water functioning as essence damage. The team also discussed visiting the midnight market for additional shopping, and addressed some technical issues with character inventory and weapon skills.

### The Midnight Market 
The group discussed weapon purchases and equipment at the midnight market, with Harry explaining that laser sights provide a +1 bonus to pistol or rifle skills. Harry added that laser sights and silencers are now available as attachments, though players must disassemble weapons with attachments before storing them in lockers. Not his fault - it's a game system thing. The team also discussed armor options, with Victor recommending the AV8 vest for its discreet nature and minimal impact on initiative. He also owns the 'Chonky' Heavy vest (AV10) for when he knows he's going into combat and doesn't need to be discrete.

### Keeper Rothman
Brian was at home when he received a spiritual visitor, Rothman, a Keeper from the Black Library. He did not visit physically: he projected himself, but could interact and move items. He asked Brian to call the other Wardens and gather them at Brian's apartment for a meeting. Keeper Rothman wanted the Wardens to investigate a missing keeper (Keeper Heinz) who unexpectedly did not show up for his library shift and did not respond to contact attempts.

There are 5 Keepers and 1 Librarian for each instance of the Black Library. There are 66 instances of the library around the world, which exist in a spiritual form in the Limen as a copy of the One True Library, in the city of Dis which exists in Hell.

### Keeper Rothman's Estate
They agreed to meet him at his physical address. It was a mansion in the nice part of town. They were let in by Bertram, the butler. They were escorted upstairs to the bedroom where Keeper Rothman (a very old man) was in a hospital bed attended by his nurse.

Rothman explained that if they wanted to visit the Library, they would need to provide personal items (blood, hair or spit) to access the library through a mirror portal in the basement. After the first trip, the portal was tuned to their essence and did not need re-tuning. 

### The Black Library
They all provided samples and were transported through the mirror portal. Once inside the library, they learned the missing keeper, Heinz, had been looking at a book about controlling ghouls. Rothman confirmed no visitors had been recorded since the previous day and no unusual behavior from Keeper Heinz had been observed. They decided to investigate Keeper Heinz's residence after learning no one was answering calls or responding to the door. 

### Keeper Heinz's Estate
The group discovered the bodies of the butler and Keeper Heinz at the mansion (both shot), with the nurse (named Marshall) still alive but with a head injury. They found a physical copy of a book that was the same as the one out of place in the library. They also discovered a map circling St. Lazarus Cemetery in East Scarborough. The group contacted Keeper Rothman about the situation and decided to stay together while searching the house, including checking the Mercedes outside which contained only muddy women's boots. They determined the nurse was the only person present at the scene, though questions remained about how she arrived and her possible involvement in the events. The nurse warned that removing the book from the Estate could have terrible consequences. Victor tested that theory and had no issues.

### Keeper Rothman's Estate and the Library
The group decided to bring Marshall (and the book) back to Keeper Rothman's place for questioning. The left her in the care of the butler while they went back into the library. Rothman examined the book that appeared to be a copy of a restricted spiritual text, and realized someone had tricked the system to create a duplicate book, which raised security concerns. By destroying the spiritual copy in the library, the physical book outside the library became blank and useless. He remarked that the knowledge in the book was not that important and could have much more easily been obtained by other means. His concern was that this was a dry-run for something bigger.

### Nurse Marshall
When the group emerged from the library, they discovered that the nurse had escaped, leaving the butler unconscious in the kitchen. They decided to pursue leads at a cemetery at night, despite the risks from ghouls, as this was where the nurse had been active. The team planned to go armed and prepared to flee if necessary. They also contacted the police about tracing the rental car's license plate. 

Harry explained that while basic rituals could potentially be performed from the book, it was a different school of magic than Clem was familiar with. Even if a voodoo book, it would take time and preparation to properly use the spells. The decided to visit the cemetery with good old fashioned weapons. Victor and Clem knew that ghouls don't need special ammo, but they can be tough and fast.

### St Lazarus Cemetery
On the way to the Cemetery they saw the **Riverside Cafe**, which Victor (not wearing his tether) noticed looked just like the Amaranth Building (all black). They decided to investigate later.

The group drove to the cemetery outside of town and found it deserted, except for many ghouls spread around the grounds, but none of them approached or threatened. The team approached the chapel where they observed figures hiding behind tombstones. They encountered a ghoul near the chapel who pointed toward the broken doors of the chapel.

### The Chapel
The group discussed their strategy for entering a chapel with ghouls, deciding that Clem, Jackson, and Brian would go inside while Blake and Victor would stay outside with rifles to cover the grounds. They discovered that the chapel had multiple entrances and approximately 8 ghouls at the far end, including what appeared to be a king ghoul. 

During a short discussion with the ghoul king, Clem offered him the foot she had been carrying around for weeks. He loved the snack and agreed to answer one question. The assassin had been there earlier and had offered the body of the real nurse Marshall as payment for information. As the group had no other body parts to offer, the ghoul king left and told the others to kill everyone.

### Chapel Combat
Lots of lead in the air for this one. Many ghouls were turned into bits. For the first time, the automated Light Special Effects tables were used, which sped up the process. Medium and Heavy Effects still need to be rolled manually and looked up in the tables. There were some close calls but no one was seriously hurt. Clem was thrown around like a rag doll. Twice. Armor is your friend.

### Summary
While they did not capture the assassin or learn the whole story, they stopped her from using the book and hopefully closed a security hole in the library. Everyone survived and learned some useful combat tactics regarding burst firing, action points, reloading, etc.
